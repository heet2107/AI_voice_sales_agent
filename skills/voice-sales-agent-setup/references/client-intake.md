# Client intake

Collect the smallest complete brief needed to configure the CRM. Prefer source URLs or client-provided documents over paraphrased assumptions.

## Business knowledge

- Legal and customer-facing business name
- Website, official contact details, service area, time zone, and operating hours
- Core services or products, customer outcomes, exclusions, and fulfillment boundaries
- Approved differentiators, evidence, testimonials, and claims
- FAQs, escalation paths, and questions the calling agent must not answer
- Business owner for approvals and the callback number or email exposed to prospects

## Agent behavior

- Truthful representative name and role
- Desired voice and tone
- Approved introduction and recording disclosure
- How to answer a direct question about AI identity
- Decision-maker roles to request
- Discovery questions and qualifying criteria
- Objections the client can support with accurate answers
- Prohibited claims, guarantees, topics, and sensitive-data categories

## Campaign design

- Inbound, outbound, or both
- Ideal customer profiles, geographies, categories, exclusions, and local buying-authority criteria
- Lead sources and the evidence required before a record is callable
- Local calling window, daily cap, retry rules, voicemail policy, and stop conditions
- DNC, consent, jurisdiction, and prior-contact requirements
- Definition of qualified interest and human handoff conditions

Treat these settings as design inputs, not permission to contact anyone.

## Scheduling and follow-up

- Scheduling system and verified booking capability
- If booking is unavailable: approved wording, destination email/mobile, and human owner for sending the link
- Pricing response and demo/inquiry-call policy
- Follow-up channels, owners, approval requirement, and service-level target

The agent must never claim a slot is booked or a link is sent without system confirmation.

## Required approvals

Record separately:

- client approval of business facts and representative identity;
- legal/compliance approval for the intended jurisdictions, consent basis, recording, and DNC process;
- technical approval of the client database and deployment targets;
- explicit authorization for any live canary or campaign.

A completed brief can make the system setup-ready. It cannot make outreach authorized by itself.
